Scholarship Tips & Advice

Master the art of tracking down funds and submitting standout applications to maximize your college awards.

How to Find Scholarships

01. Start Local First

Local scholarships—such as those offered by Pea Ridge community groups, local businesses, and regional credit unions—have significantly fewer applicants than national awards, giving you a much higher statistical chance of winning.

02. Leverage the Hub's Filters

Use the search and filter features on the Scholarships page efficiently. Search by keywords like "no-essay", "all-students", or filter by monetary tiers to target awards that match your specific situation.

03. Check Institutional Aid

Look directly at the financial aid and scholarship pages of the specific colleges or universities you are planning to apply to. Many schools offer automatic consideration based on GPA and test scores.

04. Broaden Your Search Criteria

Don't just search by your intended major. Look for opportunities based on your hobbies, parent employers, ethnic background, community service involvement, or unique life experiences.

How to Apply Successfully

01. Track Deadlines Closely

Create a calendar dedicated exclusively to scholarship due dates. Missing a deadline by even a few minutes means immediate disqualification, no matter how strong your submission is.

02. Tailor Your Essays

Never copy-paste a generic essay for every application. Read the prompt carefully and customize your response to reflect the specific values, mission, or goals of the organization giving out the award.

03. Secure Recommendations Early

Give your teachers, counselors, or mentors at least 2–3 weeks notice before asking them for a letter of recommendation. Provide them with a brief summary of your goals and accomplishments to help them write a stronger letter.

04. Proofread Everything

Typos, grammatical errors, and incorrect formatting can sink an otherwise stellar application. Always review your work multiple times and have a trusted peer or teacher look it over before hitting submit.
