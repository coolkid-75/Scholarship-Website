About This Scholarship

The Burger King Scholars Program provides scholarships to high school seniors, Burger King employees, and their families to help cover college or post-secondary vocational/technical education. Awards are based on factors including academics, work experience, extracurricular activities, financial need, and community service.

Eligibility

1. Must be a high school senior or Burger King employee or family member. 2. Must be pursuing a college or post-secondary vocational/technical education. 3. Must demonstrate academic achievement, work experience, extracurricular involvement, financial need, and/or community service.
