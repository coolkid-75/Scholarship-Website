Washington Crossing Foundation Scholarship

Award $5,000 [31 Awards]
Deadline January 15, 2027

About This Scholarship

The Washington Crossing Foundation Scholarship supports high school seniors pursuing careers in government service, honoring leadership, public service, and the legacy of George Washington.

Eligibility

1. Applicant must be a high school senior completing twelfth grade or the final year of high school in the specified year. 2. Applicant must be a citizen of the United States. 3. Applicant must plan to pursue a career serving the United States through local, state, or federal government. 4. Applicant must submit a one-page essay explaining their interest in a government service career, including any inspiration drawn from George Washington's leadership during the crossing of the Delaware. 5. Applicant must obtain a recommendation from their high school principal or guidance counselor evaluating their achievements. 6. Applicant must submit academic transcripts. 7. Applicant must submit national testing scores. 8. Applicant may include additional supporting information of their choice, as described in the official scholarship rules. 9. Applicant must submit three copies of a double-spaced cover page containing all required information, including personal contact details, school information, class and school enrollment numbers, recommender information, preferred colleges, scholarship discovery information, high school senior status, date of birth, and applicable regional scholarship interests. 10. Applicants seeking regional scholarships must meet the residency or other applicable regional requirements: the West Coast Laura Daniel Brown Scholarship is for residents of Alaska, Arizona, California, Hawaii, Idaho, Montana, Nevada, New Mexico, Oregon, Utah, Washington, or Wyoming; the Charlie Kirk Memorial Scholarship gives preference to Utah residents or students committing to attend a Utah secondary school in person for eight semesters; the Pennsylvania Scholarship is for Pennsylvania residents; the L. John Hutton Scholarship gives preference to Indiana residents; the Charles E. Tweedy Scholarship gives preference to Alabama residents or students committing to attend an Alabama secondary school in person for at least two semesters; and the Eichelberger Southern Maryland Scholarship is for Southern Maryland residents. 11. Applicants planning to attend a military academy or who have received a full four-year tuition scholarship may still be eligible for an award. 12. Scholarship recipients must meet the requirements of their chosen college. 13. Winners must attend the scholarship awards ceremony and luncheon to receive their scholarship.
