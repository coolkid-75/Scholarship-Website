Automotive Women's Association Scholarships

Award $5,000 [22 Awards]
Deadline January 2, 2027

About This Scholarship

The Automotive Women's Alliance Foundation Scholarship supports women pursuing careers in the automotive, vehicular, and mobility industries by helping fund their college education and professional development.

Eligibility

1. Applicant must be accepted to or enrolled in an accredited college or university. 2. Applicant must maintain a minimum GPA of 3.0 during a scholarship-funded semester. 3. Applicant must be a citizen of a North American country. 4. Applicant must demonstrate career aspirations in the automotive, vehicular, or mobility industries. 5. Applicant must complete the online scholarship application. 6. Applicant must submit a one-page cover letter detailing their career aspirations in the automotive, vehicular, or mobility industries, including their name. 7. Applicant must submit a resume of no more than two pages detailing education, work experience, volunteer activities, and extracurricular activities. 8. Applicant must submit an academic transcript; high school seniors must provide a high school transcript, while other applicants must provide their current or most recent college transcript. 9. High school seniors and college transfer students who are not yet enrolled must provide an acceptance letter from their college or school. 10. All required documents must be legible and submitted in PDF, PNG, or JPG format, with each file smaller than 5 MB. 11. Applicant must provide written or electronic confirmation of scholarship acceptance within five days of receiving the award notification. 12. Applicant must notify the AWAF Scholarship Committee if they cannot accept the scholarship or if their education is interrupted, as the status of the funds will then be reviewed. 13. Applicants must be willing to participate in an interview if requested by the AWAF Scholarship Committee. 14. Recommendation letters and other noteworthy supporting materials may be submitted but are optional.
