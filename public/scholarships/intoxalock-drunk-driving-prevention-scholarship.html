Intoxalock Drunk Driving Prevention Scholarship

Award $3,000 [1 Award]
Deadline February 28, 2027

About This Scholarship

The Intoxalock Drunk Driving Prevention Scholarship supports students who raise awareness about drunk driving prevention through written responses and a public service announcement video highlighting roadway safety.

Eligibility

1. Applicant must be currently enrolled or accepted for enrollment at an accredited two-year or four-year U.S. college, university, or trade/vocational program. 2. Applicants may pursue any field of study. 3. Applicant must provide written responses, including a personal introduction, an impact statement, academic information, and an explanation of how the scholarship will be used. 4. Applicant must create a public service announcement video addressing drunk driving prevention. 5. The video must be in vertical format and between 60 and 90 seconds long. 6. Applicant must appear on camera with their face visible and well-framed throughout the video; voice-over-only submissions are not accepted. 7. The video must have clear audio and a clean, well-lit background. 8. Applicant must mention Intoxalock by name on camera and showcase the Intoxalock device using the provided brand resources. 9. The video must address the second-chance message, acknowledging how Intoxalock helps offenders regain driving privileges responsibly. 10. The video must explain how ignition interlock technology helps prevent drunk driving and protect lives. 11. The video must be exported as an MP4 file at 720p resolution or higher. 12. Applicant must provide the full legal name of their educational institution, current grade level, most recent cumulative GPA on a 4.0 scale or equivalent, degree or program focus, and an explanation of how the scholarship will support their educational expenses. 13. Applicant must provide written responses explaining their background, values, motivations, personal connection to drunk driving prevention, the impact of drunk driving on their perspective, and their views on ignition interlock technology and roadway safety. 14. Applicant must provide an active email address and phone number where they can be contacted if selected as a finalist. 15. All written responses must be proofread for grammar, clarity, and spelling. 16. Applicants must submit a complete application, including all required written responses and the video submission. 17. Applicants must be willing to participate in public social media voting if selected as a finalist, as the final selection considers both internal judging scores and public engagement. 18. The video must directly address drunk driving prevention, demonstrate thoughtfulness and originality, communicate an effective message, acknowledge the second-chance message, and explain how ignition interlock technology saves lives.
