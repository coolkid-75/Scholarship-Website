Kennedy Center VSA Young Soloists Award

Award $2,500 [4 Awards]
Deadline November 19, 2026

About This Scholarship

The Kennedy Center VSA Young Soloists Award supports young musicians with disabilities through financial assistance, performance opportunities, mentorship, and professional development across musical genres.

Eligibility

1. Must be a musician with a disability. 2. Must be between 14 and 25 years old at the time of the application deadline. 3. Must be a U.S. citizen, U.S. legal resident, or international applicant; applicants from other countries may also apply. 4. Must not have previously won the Access/VSA International Young Musicians Program. 5. Must be willing to participate in all required workshops, rehearsals, and performances if selected. 6. Finalists must provide proof of age and disability. 7. U.S. applicants selected as finalists must provide proof of authorization to work in the United States. 8. Winners must agree to publicly disclose their disability in program-related materials and public appearances. 9. Must be able to participate in program activities conducted in English and/or American Sign Language (ASL).
