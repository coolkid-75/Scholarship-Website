SCHOLARSHIP OPPORTUNITY

C-Span StudentCam Competition

Award $5,000 [150 Awards]
Deadline January 20, 2027

About This Scholarship

The C-SPAN StudentCam Competition invites students in grades 6–12 to create documentaries examining a national or community issue, exploring multiple viewpoints and potential solutions.

Eligibility

1. All students in grades 6-12. 2. Grades 6-8 compete in the Middle School category, and grades 9-12 compete in the High School category. 3. Students may compete individually, or in teams of either 2 or 3 members. 4. Documentaries must include clips of C-SPAN video that relate to the chosen topic. 5. Winning StudentCam documentaries will thoroughly explore a variety of viewpoints related to the chosen topic, including those that may oppose the filmmakers points of view. 6. Your documentary must be between 5:00-6:00 minutes in length. 7. Entries must include either end credits or a works cited list.
