Kochhar & Co. Scholarship

Award $1,000 [1 Award]
Deadline March 31, 2027

About This Scholarship

The Kochhar & Co. Scholarship supports students across various fields of study while encouraging them to explore the important role accountants play in supporting companies or individuals through a structured essay.

Eligibility

1. Attend college or university on a full-time or part-time basis no later than September 2027. 2. Have achieved a GPA of 2.5 or greater during the last academic year. 3. Be a resident of Canada or the United States. 4. Submit an essay answering exactly one of the two provided questions. 5. Keep the essay under 1,000 words. 6. Submit the essay by 11:59 PM EST on the application deadline. 7. Follow basic formatting and grammar rules. 8. Include an introductory paragraph, several supporting body paragraphs, and a concluding paragraph. 9. Properly cite any quoted sources using MLA or APA formatting.
