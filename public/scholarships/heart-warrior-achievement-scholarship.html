Heart Warrior Achievement Scholarship

Award $5,000 [15 Awards]
Deadline January 1, 2027

About This Scholarship

A scholarship supporting students with congenital heart disease as they pursue higher education and demonstrate perseverance through their personal experiences.

Eligibility

1. A high school senior in the United States or a student currently enrolled in a U.S. college program. 2. A student who has been diagnosed with congenital heart disease and is receiving care for the condition. 3. A student preparing to begin or continue postsecondary education at a college, university, or trade school. 4. An applicant who provides a letter from a healthcare practitioner confirming their congenital heart disease diagnosis and care. 5. An applicant who provides an academic transcript or proof of enrollment. 6. An applicant who provides a professional or academic letter of reference from someone who is not related to them. 7. An applicant who shares their personal experience with congenital heart disease, including how their journey has shaped their perseverance, learning, or goals.
