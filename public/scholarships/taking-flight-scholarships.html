Taking Flight Scholarships

Award $5,000 [7 Awards]
Deadline October 30, 2026

About This Scholarship

The Taking Flight Grants support Indigenous students pursuing education and training for careers connected to mineral extraction and processing. The program encourages students to explore opportunities in mining, trades, engineering, environmental studies, and other fields supporting mining communities.

Eligibility

1. Be Indigenous, including First Nations, Métis, or Inuit. 2. Be a permanent resident of Canada. 3. Be pursuing or planning to pursue studies related to the mineral extraction and processing industries. 4. Be a high school graduate continuing with a short course, completing high school requirements as an adult, attending college or CEGEP, attending trade school, or attending university. 5. Submit a completed application by the October deadline. 6. Provide the most recent academic transcript, with an unofficial transcript accepted. 7. Provide two recommendation and/or reference letters from teachers, Elders, or community leaders. 8. Submit a 400-word letter in English or the applicant's native language describing what working in the mining industry means to them and their significant accomplishments.
