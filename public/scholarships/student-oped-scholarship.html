Student OpEd Scholarship

Award $5,000 [3 Awards]
Deadline December 5, 2026

About This Scholarship

The Student OpEd Scholarship supports enrolled college students who develop original, well-researched arguments about infrastructure challenges. Applicants must write an OpEd based on the provided prompt, incorporate Aii research, and submit the article to a publication.

Eligibility

1. Must be a currently enrolled college or graduate student in the United States. 2. Must submit only one entry. 3. Must write an original OpEd of no more than 900 words addressing the prompt. 4. Must cite Aii research and materials in the article. 5. Must submit the article to a local, state, or national publication. 6. Must provide a published article link or proof of a good-faith submission attempt.
