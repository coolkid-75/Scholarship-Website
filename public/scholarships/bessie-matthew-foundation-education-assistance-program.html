SCHOLARSHIP OPPORTUNITY

Bessie Mathew Foundation Education Assistance Program

Award $5,000 [3 Awards]
Deadline November 30, 2026

About This Scholarship

The Bessie Mathew Foundation Education Assistance Program helps families affected by cancer manage education-related expenses, including childcare, school supplies, tutoring, and college costs.

Eligibility

1. At least one member of the applicant's household must have been diagnosed with cancer. 2. The household must include at least one female. 3. A household member must have an education-related financial need, including tuition, tutoring, uniforms, books, or other educational expenses. 4. Eligible educational programs include daycare, after-school care, pre-kindergarten through 12th grade, and accredited colleges or universities located in the United States. 5. Applicant must reside in the United States or a U.S. territory. 6. Applicant must not have received a Bessie Mathew Foundation Education Assistance Program payment within the past 12 months. 7. Applicant must not be an immediate family member of the Bessie Mathew Foundation board or selection committee. 8. Applicant must be able to provide documentation of the costs requiring financial assistance, such as an invoice. 9. Requested assistance must cover future educational expenses; previously paid expenses are not eligible for reimbursement.
