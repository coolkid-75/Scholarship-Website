Excellence in Ag Scholarship

Award $16,000 [4 Awards]
Deadline November 15, 2026

About This Scholarship

The Excellence in Ag Program supports students preparing for careers in agriculture and agribusiness through educational funding, practical industry experience, and opportunities to develop professional skills and connections.

Eligibility

1. Be entering or already enrolled in a diploma or degree program in agriculture or business. 2. Attend an accredited post-secondary institution. 3. Submit a completed and signed application form. 4. Submit a resume. 5. Provide proof of post-secondary enrollment or a conditional acceptance letter. 6. Submit an academic transcript. 7. Provide two reference letters from a teacher, principal, guidance counselor, supervisor, or mentor. 8. At least one reference must be from a work supervisor. 9. Submit a 500-word essay or video addressing one of the three specified topics: co-operatives and their impact on local communities, the future of agriculture in Western Canada, or a great coach/teacher and their leadership or mentorship qualities.
