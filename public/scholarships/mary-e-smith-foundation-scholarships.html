SCHOLARSHIP OPPORTUNITY

Mary E. Smith Foundation Scholarships

Award $2,500 [4 Awards]
Deadline February 1, 2027

About This Scholarship

The Mary E. Smith Foundation offers scholarships supporting students affected by brain tumors, aspiring healthcare professionals, and Chicago Public School students pursuing science-related careers.

Eligibility

1. National Excellence in Academics & Community Service Award: Applicant must be a brain tumor patient or survivor, or closely related to someone diagnosed with a brain tumor. 2. Eligible family relationships for the National Excellence in Academics & Community Service Award are limited to spouses, biological children, biological parents, and biological siblings. 3. Applicants must be U.S. residents. 4. Applicants must be entering or currently enrolled in an advanced educational program at a college, university, vocational school, or other educational institution located within the 50 United States or the District of Columbia. 5. Applicants must demonstrate a need for financial assistance. 6. National Excellence in Science & Medicine Award: Applicant must be a U.S. resident entering or currently enrolled in an advanced health sciences educational program, including biology, chemistry, or nursing. 7. Applicants for the National Excellence in Science & Medicine Award must intend to pursue a career in oncology or neurology, including neuroscience, neurosurgery, or neuropathology. 8. Applicants for the National Excellence in Science & Medicine Award must attend a college or four-year university located within the 50 United States or the District of Columbia. 9. Karen Lewis CTU-CPS Excellence in Science Award: Applicant must be a graduate or current student of the Chicago Public Schools (CPS). 10. Applicants for the Karen Lewis CTU-CPS Excellence in Science Award must be entering or currently enrolled in an advanced educational program in health sciences, including biology, chemistry, or nursing. 11. Applicants for the Karen Lewis CTU-CPS Excellence in Science Award must be pursuing a technical certification, associate degree, or bachelor's degree. 12. Applicants for the Karen Lewis CTU-CPS Excellence in Science Award must focus their studies on science, screening, or research in oncology or neurology, including neuroscience, neurosurgery, or neuropathology. 13. Applicants for the Karen Lewis CTU-CPS Excellence in Science Award must attend a college or four-year university located within the 50 United States or the District of Columbia. 14. #GIVING TUESDAY Award: Applicant must have previously applied for one of the foundation's scholarships. 15. Applicants for the #GIVING TUESDAY Award must be pursuing or planning to pursue a career in health sciences. 16. Applicants for the #GIVING TUESDAY Award must provide verification of enrollment in a college, medical school, or technical certification program for the applicable academic term.
