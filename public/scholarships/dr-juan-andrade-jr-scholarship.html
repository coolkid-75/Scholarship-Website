Dr Juan Andrade Jr. Scholarship

Award $1,000 [20 Awards]
Deadline December 30, 2026

About This Scholarship

The Dr. Juan Andrade Jr. Scholarship supports undergraduate students through financial assistance while encouraging leadership development, academic achievement, and community engagement.

Eligibility

1. Applicant must be enrolled or accepted for enrollment as a full-time undergraduate student at an accredited two-year or four-year institution in the United States or its territories. 2. Applicant must demonstrate a verifiable financial need. 3. Applicants of any citizenship status, including non-U.S. citizens, may apply. 4. Applicant must be a full-time undergraduate student during the specified academic year. 5. Eligible applicants include high school seniors, college freshmen, sophomores, and juniors up to age 25. 6. Applicant must submit a 500–1,000-word personal essay describing their background, family history, life or work experiences, significant achievements, and any obstacles they have overcome. 7. Applicant must submit a 500–1,000-word leadership essay discussing their vision for America's future, the leadership needed, their intended leadership role, potential community benefits, and how USHLI can help them achieve their goals. 8. Applicant may submit an optional essay sharing additional relevant information supporting their application. 9. Applicant must submit a recent, high-resolution, color portrait photograph in PNG, JPG, or GIF format, with a 2.5:3.5 aspect ratio, suitable for printed publications; selfies are not accepted. 10. Applicant must submit a one-page resume in PDF format detailing work experience, academic accomplishments, and other relevant information. 11. Applicant must submit their most recent unofficial academic transcript in PDF format; college freshmen must submit their high school transcript. 12. Applicant must submit two letters of recommendation focusing on leadership abilities, academic performance, and the ability or desire to serve the community. 13. One recommendation must come from an academic reference, such as a teacher or academic advisor. 14. One recommendation must come from a professional or community service reference, such as a supervisor, employer, or coach. 15. Applicant must provide contact information for both references through the application. 16. Applicant must ensure both recommendation letters are submitted by the references according to the scholarship's instructions.
