SCHOLARSHIP OPPORTUNITY

ExploraVision Awards

Award $10,000 [40 Awards]
Deadline January 27, 2027

About This Scholarship

The ExploraVision competition challenges K-12 students to collaboratively research current technologies and envision how they could evolve into innovative solutions for real-world problems 10 or more years ahead.

Eligibility

1. All students must be United States or Canadian citizens and living within the United States or Canada, U.S. Territories, or a Department of War Education Activity (DoWEA) school abroad, and enrolled full-time in a public, private, parochial, virtual school, or home school. 2. Students must be no older than 21 years of age. 3. National Science Teaching Association employees, NSTA board members, ExploraVision judges and their respective families are not eligible to enter the competition. 4. Any student who was a regional or national finalist in ExploraVision can only compete in subsequent years with a new team — i.e., with students who have not previously been selected as ExploraVision regional or national finalists. 5. Students enter as a team of 2-4 students with a teacher/coach and optional mentor (no individual entries). 6. Students may only submit one project per year. However, each teacher/coach can participate in multiple projects per year. 7. A coach must be at least 21 years of age. 8. Coaches may be one of the following: a teacher, coach, counselor, leader in a youth organization, or parent/guardian. 9. A coach must know at least one student on the team. 10. A coach cannot be a judge in the same competition year. 11. Any project that has won at ExploraVision’s regional and/or national level may not be resubmitted in future years. 12. Any project previously awarded a prize in another competition may not be submitted. 13. AI and the tool ChatGPT may be used to brainstorm and/or research your project ideas. These should be used as a tool, but not as the only resource for your project. Any information generated by these resources must be included in the bibliography with the proper citations from the original resources. 14. ExploraVision submissions must adhere to the AI policy. Access the AI Acceptable Use Chart for examples.
