Referee and Umpire Leadership Scholarship

Award $2,500 [1 Award]
Deadline December 15, 2026

About This Scholarship

The Referee and Umpire Leadership Scholarship recognizes students who have developed leadership skills through youth sports officiating and encourages continued leadership in education and community life.

Eligibility

1. Be enrolled in high school, college, trade school, university, or graduate school. 2. Reside in the United States. 3. Be in good academic standing. 4. Have worked as a referee or umpire before attending the eligible educational institution. 5. Submit the required personal and academic information. 6. Author and submit an original essay addressing the scholarship topic. 7. Ensure the essay meets the required word count. 8. Not be an employee or child of an employee of BenGlassLaw. 9. Not be a previous recipient of the Referee and Umpire Leadership Scholarship. 10. Be willing to participate in an interview with a BenGlassLaw representative if selected. 11. Not submit the same essay for multiple scholarships or multiple years. 12. Agree to the required release allowing BenGlassLaw to use the applicant's name, image, likeness, and essay for marketing and social media purposes. 13. Not use generative AI to create the submitted essay.
