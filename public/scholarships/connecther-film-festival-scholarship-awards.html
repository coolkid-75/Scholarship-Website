ConnectHER Film Festival Scholarship Awards

Award $5,000 [12 Awards]
Deadline October 1, 2026

About This Scholarship

The ConnectHER Film Festival is an international youth competition that welcomes short films about issues that impact women and girls.

Eligibility

1. All filmmakers must be 13–25 at submission. 2. Film must address an issue impacting women and girls and fit an approved topic area. 3. Film must be an original work not previously screened at a major film festival. 4. Film must be completed after January 1, 2026. 5. Film must be 3–6 minutes long. 6. Film must include English subtitles. 7. Film must highlight two organizations working on the issue addressed. 8. Submission must be received by the stated deadline; late entries are not accepted.
