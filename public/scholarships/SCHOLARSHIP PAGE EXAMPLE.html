Army Women's Foundation Scholarship

Scholarship opportunity for women who served honorably in the U.S. Army and eligible descendants.

Award Varies
Deadline January 21, 2026
Education College / Graduate / Vocational
Eligibility Army Women & Descendants

About This Scholarship

The Army Women's Foundation Scholarship is available to women who served honorably in the U.S. Army or their lineal descendants.

Students may attend an accredited college, university, community college, vocational school, or graduate program.

What You Should Know

Award amounts and the number of scholarships can vary depending on the category, including certificate, associate, undergraduate, and graduate programs.
